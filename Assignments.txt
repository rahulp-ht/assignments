# Practical Assignment

The following assignment is divided into modules. Complete the topics and hands-on activities under each module.

For each module, you are required to:

* Complete all listed hands-on activities.
* Understand the concepts behind each task.
* Record a video demonstrating your implementation.
* Explain what you implemented, why you implemented it, and how it works.
* Share the video along with required screenshots/evidence.
